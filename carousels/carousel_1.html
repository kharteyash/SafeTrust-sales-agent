New Construction Math

New homes are cheaper than existing ones

New builds are actually undercutting existing home prices right now.

What Happened

Builder pricing flips the traditional market script

Buyers typically expect to pay a premium for brand new construction over existing homes. Right now, that math is turned upside down as new build prices drop below resale homes.

Resale sellers are stuck in the past

Existing homeowners are anchoring to peak valuation numbers from previous years. Builders operate on cash flow and clear inventory fast by cutting base prices aggressively.

What this changes in underwriting

Here is what this actually changes when a file hits underwriting: builder price cuts and incentives directly lower DTI (debt-to-income, the percent of income buying debt) without appraisal hurdles.

Rate buydowns shift the monthly payment

Builders use scale to subsidize financing instead of holding firm on price. That structured financing lowers the buyer's PITI (principal, interest, taxes, and insurance) far more effectively.

Clean approvals on new builds
